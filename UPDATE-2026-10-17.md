## Implemented

The new World 7 Menagerie page has eight views: Missions, Unlocks, Boosts, Jellyfish, Singularity, Scurge, Nightmariners, and Lumi sources. It reads the exported Menagerie arrays, keeps absent values unknown, and uses native boost/unlock prices and rank requirements. All 33 mission definitions are shown, including those beyond the 25 mentioned in the patch notes.

Existing catalogs and collection displays include the new card, vial, map name, equipment and Slab entries, lore chapter, white Legend Talents, Atom, Research squares, Jade purchase, Zenith upgrade, Gaming upgrade, Gilded Coral, Luminous Shell, Bloodclot, and seventh artifact tier. Arcade saved level 102 uses the native sixfold bonus. Coin formatting supports all 30 denominations.

Calculation changes include Jelly and Sushi upgrade doublers, Bloodclot and white-talent Jelly damage, Summoning discounts, Scurge pearl modifiers, and Menagerie multipliers in shared Research EXP, cooking speed/mastery, class EXP, drop rate, Spelunking power, and land-rank EXP. Shared parsers also include the meal/atom/Legend Talent cap changes. No Meal Left Behind stops at meal level 150.

## Coverage limits

- This is not a complete simulation of every new game interaction. Lumi sources are a checklist, not a computed account-wide Lumi/hour value.
- Missions that require unavailable live state remain unknown. The tracker does not claim that an unlocked slot or in-game claim button is available merely because a numeric target is reached.
- Crucible combat, side-jelly actions, organic-label allocation, black-star allocation, and automatic ladle use are represented by unlock/boost tracking; their full interactive planners are not implemented.
- Several existing features use a separate older bundled engine. Catalogs are refreshed there, but full propagation of all Menagerie effects through every optimizer still needs a dedicated dependency audit.
- No live post-update account export was supplied. Validation uses the existing account fixture plus synthetic Menagerie records.
