Source: the user-supplied Steam `resources/app.asar`, extracted on 2026-10-17. The extracted N.js SHA-256 is `a2657944c1f9e2dda4d58ed57ef2ede60d139078450fd536a5fb7bbb40b873a2`. The previous extraction is preserved in `../audit/N-before-2026-10-17.js`.

## Implemented

The new World 7 Menagerie page has nine views: Missions, Unlocks, Boosts, Jellyfish, Singularity, Scurge, Nightmariners, Crucible, and Lumi sources. It reads the exported Menagerie arrays, keeps absent values unknown, and uses native boost/unlock prices and rank requirements. All 33 mission definitions are shown, including those beyond the 25 mentioned in the patch notes.

The follow-up adds account Lumi/hour with each source, the next Lumi boost's marginal gain and payback, mission slot gating, more mission counters, all 16 Crucible milestones, pink/yellow jellyfish upgrade prices, and Scurge EXP requirements. The Lumi worker suppresses a rate when required source records are absent and ignores stale results after another save is loaded.

Older calculations now include Menagerie discounts for Orion/Poppy/Bubba, Forge speed, Sailing loot and artifact factors, Nightmariners, shiny-critter bundle size, Gaming black stars, Farming organic labels, Minehead doublers/white talent, shrine caps, and yellow-jelly Construction EXP. Shiny pets and their milestone planner support level 30 after the unlock; shared shiny-speed calculations include overlevels. The legacy decoder installs bonus hooks before its dependency passes. Older errors corrected alongside this work include the Godshard Forge passive, Sailing captain loot grouping/base loot formula, and the Minehead Arcade multiplier group.

Existing catalogs and collection displays include the new card, vial, map name, equipment and Slab entries, lore chapter, white Legend Talents, Atom, Research squares, Jade purchase, Zenith upgrade, Gaming upgrade, Gilded Coral, Luminous Shell, Bloodclot, and seventh artifact tier. Arcade saved level 102 uses the native sixfold bonus. Coin formatting supports all 30 denominations.

Calculation changes include Jelly and Sushi upgrade doublers, Bloodclot and white-talent Jelly damage, Summoning discounts, Scurge pearl modifiers, and Menagerie multipliers in shared Research EXP, cooking speed/mastery, class EXP, drop rate, Spelunking power, and land-rank EXP. Shared parsers also include the meal/atom/Legend Talent cap changes. No Meal Left Behind stops at meal level 150.

## Coverage limits

- Missions without a verified complete reconstruction remain unknown. A reached target within the unlocked slots is a progress check, not confirmation of an in-game claim button.
- Crucible wave rewards and side-jelly costs are calculated; combat simulation, automatic allocation of labels/stars, and automatic ladle scheduling are not implemented. Imported label/star assignments affect existing calculators.
- Summoning upgrade discounts are implemented; a complete account essence-per-hour calculator is not part of this pass. The legacy engine remains a separate implementation and is not claimed to have universal parity for every game formula.
- No live post-update account export was supplied. Validation uses the existing account fixture plus synthetic Menagerie records.
