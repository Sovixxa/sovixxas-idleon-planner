Privacy & analytics

Google advertising

The homepage and planner use Google AdSense. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Advertising cookies can personalize ads based on your visits to this site and other websites.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings and find opt-out options for participating third-party advertising vendors at YourAdChoices. The analytics control below applies to planner analytics, not Google advertising.

Planner data & analytics

Google sign-in uses Idleon’s device-code flow. You enter a code and approve access on Google’s website; the planner never asks for your Google password. The browser requests basic email/profile sign-in access directly from Google and exchanges the resulting ID token with Idleon’s Firebase Authentication. Pending codes are kept in memory and cleared when sign-in finishes or is cancelled. Google access and refresh tokens are not saved by the planner.

Optional cloud sync signs into Idleon’s Firebase services and reads your account’s cloud save, character names, companions, guild information, and server settings. Email credentials are sent directly to Firebase Authentication. Steam sign-in happens on Steam’s website; the pasted redirect is exchanged directly with Idleon’s sign-in service. The planner does not send these credentials or your save to a planner backend or analytics.

Firebase libraries are loaded from Google’s CDN when you connect. Firebase keeps authentication tokens for this tab by default. If you select Keep me signed in on this device, Firebase stores them persistently in this browser until you disconnect or the session is revoked. The planner clears password and Steam redirect fields after submission and never stores them itself. Disconnect stops updates and signs out, while keeping the displayed save in memory. The existing save-preservation checkbox separately controls whether a save copy survives a tab reload. Your existing local notes, goals, and import comparison summaries remain in browser storage.

Your imported save is processed in your browser. The planner’s analytics integration does not send save contents, character names, pasted text, or error messages to Umami.

When configured, Umami receives anonymous page visits, selected action outcomes, generic error counts, browser language, screen size, referring website origin, and estimated active time. Requests also expose your IP address to the analytics service as part of normal internet communication. No account identifier or session recording is enabled by this integration.

Active time is estimated while the tab is visible and within one minute of interaction. It is not an exact measure of attention. Analytics honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.
