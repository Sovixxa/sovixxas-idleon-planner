Simulation model / caveats

The operation loop is simulated on the same 1/60-second logical timer used by the shipped client. Cell attack progress, projectile travel, Amoeba weakening stacks, Organelle speed, Fever, Critical Condition square-by-square deaths, Immunoid aggro, Steroid timing and revives are modeled as timed events. Multi-square cells keep attacking until their anchor square dies, matching the shipped client.

Account-wide damage sources outside the Jelly blob still need calibration. Leave “Observed current clear” blank to use the saved best-DPS fallback, or enter the clear time you just observed with the pasted current board to anchor the model to your live game state.

Random initial attack progress and Critical Condition target selection are Monte Carlo simulated, so the optimizer compares clear probability and clear-time distributions instead of one fake-perfect DPS number. If Stronkroid is unlocked, the solver also searches when to press it during the operation.
